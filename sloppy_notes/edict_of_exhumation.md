THE EDICT OF EXHUMATION
An Imperial Judicial Decree and Commission Mandate issued by the Imperial Commission of Old Ebonheart, Third Era, Year 421.
------------------------------
## PREAMBLE & JURISDICTION
BE IT KNOWN to all Dunmer citizens, House Lords, and Prelates of the Tribunal Temple, that this Commission, by the sovereign authority vested in the Office of the Duke of Morrowind and the Supreme Magistrate of the Imperial Courts under the provisions of the Armistice of 2E 896, does hereby issue this definitive decree.
This ruling concludes the consolidated mass-action suit filed by the Guild of Barristers (Old Ebonheart District) on behalf of thirty-four hundred recognized Dunmer clans, minor estate-holders, and joint Great House lineages (The Houses Allied v. Archcanonry of Vivec).
## THE FINDING OF INFRACEMENT
Upon exhaustive review of pre-Imperial treaties and the structural codicils of the Armistice, this Court finds that the Tribunal Temple has systematically committed Massive Breach of Testamentary Contract and Retrospective Asset Seizure across the mainland and district territories.
While Section III of the Armistice strictly protects the traditional practice of Dunmer Ancestor Worship, the creation of the Great Ghostfence (circa 3E 400) constitutes an unprecedented state utility. By retroactively exhuming, conscripting, and repurposing the physical bone ash and spirits of Dunmer citizens interred between 2E 896 and 3E 400 without explicit, written familial consent, the Temple has unlawfully violated the posthumous property rights guaranteed under Imperial statutory law.
Furthermore, by stripping local Ancestral Tombs of their legally sanctioned guardian spirits to power the outer Rim-Fence, the Temple has left private estates defenseless, causing catastrophic depreciation of land value and rampant secular grave-robbing.
## THE ORDERS OF THE COURT
THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED AND DECREED:

* THE VOLITIONAL GRANDFATHER CLAUSE: The state-mandated conscription of any spirit interred prior to the formal declaration of the Ghostfence Mandate (3E 400) is declared null and void. The remains of these individuals are recognized as the exclusive, private property of their surviving blood descendants.
* CEASE AND DESIST ORDER: The Archcanonry and its subordinate Temples must immediately cease the extraction of spiritual frequencies from the grand-class crypts of the class claimants for the purpose of powering the Red Mountain containment barrier.
* MANDATORY SPIRITUAL DECOUPLING: The Keepers of the Dead at Necrom and the Master Waking Guides at the Ghostgate machinery are legally compelled to systematically untangle, separate, and isolate the distinct ancestral ash-vibrations of the forty-two thousand verified claimants currently bound to the barrier.

## METHOD OF EXECUTION
The Tribunal Temple is granted a period of no more than two winters to execute the physical and spiritual return of the property.

* The Ash Caravans: All decoupled remains must be placed into sanctified, lead-lined urns, verified by an appointed Imperial Magistrate and a Guild Barrister, and escorted via Temple caravan back to their ancestral plots at the Temple's sole expense.
* The Right of Local Animation: Upon return to their native soil, families retain the full, unabridged right to perform "Sacred Necromancy" to bind these returned ancestors to their local vaults as tomb-guardians, completely free from state interference or conscription.

## PENALTY FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
Failure by any Temple prelate, Ordinator, or Buoyant Armiger to facilitate the immediate release of a grandfathered soul shall be viewed as an act of open defiance against the Emperor’s Peace. Such acts will result in the immediate forfeiture of all Imperial trade subsidies to the offending district Temple and the deployment of the Imperial Legion to enforce exhumation by physical decree.
