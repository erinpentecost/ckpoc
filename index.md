## People

### Feldrelo Sadri (Vanilla)
Balmora Temple Master. Very formal, but stressed. She gives you `ash interment` quests, and can admit you to the `Keepers of the Dead`. Once all her deliviers are done, she'll direct the player to the Ghostgate to seek out the `Keepers of the Dead` representative there.

Over the course of the deliveries, the player will encounter two tombs filled with Daedra and one filled with vampires. These could spawn additional quests, and she can direct you to the Ghostgate to report on the status of the desecrated tombs.

### Boran Andules (New)
The head Keeper representative in Vvardenfell. Rude, terse, and overworked. He's at the Ghostgate. He can admit you to the `Keepers of the Dead`.

He'll direct the player to help out `Feldrelo Sadri`.

He can direct the player to Molag Mar.
